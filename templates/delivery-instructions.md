## 1 · Logo
- **Which version?**
  - **Full** is the default.
  - **Monogram** when the logo is narrower than ~120 px or 32 mm.
- **Which colour?**
  - **Dark** on light backgrounds.
  - **Light** on dark backgrounds.
  - **Black/white** for one-colour print only.
- **Which format?**
  - **SVG** for web and design tools.
  - **PNG @1x/@2x/@4x** when SVG isn't accepted.
  - **PDF/EPS** for printers.
  - **Favicons** at 32, 180 and 512 px.
- **Don't** stretch, recolour, add shadows or frames, or place the logo on busy photos. Keep clear space of {{rule}}.
